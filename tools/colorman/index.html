Help & Shortcuts

Layout

Library (left)
Your palettes, saved colors, recently used colors, and all 148 named CSS colors. Click any color to make it the active color.
Workbench (center)
The active color: its picker, every format it can be written in, and the working palette you are building.
Tools (right)
Adjust, check, and extend the active color: contrast, harmonies, shades, mixing, gradients, color-vision previews, and export.

Click a pane's title to open it; one pane per side is open at a time. Drag a title to reorder the panes. On a phone, the two buttons at the top right slide each side in.

Workbench

Color field
Type or paste any color; it applies as you type. Enter keeps it and rewrites it in your primary format, Escape goes back to the color you had before the edit. The whole edit is one undo step, and the color joins Recent when you press Enter or leave the field. If colorMan can't read it, a note under the field says why and nothing changes.
Toolbar
Click the color chip to copy the color. The buttons pick from the screen, give a random color, undo or redo a color change, copy, save the color to Saved Colors (star), or add it to the working palette.
Picker
Drag in the color area or slide the hue and opacity bars. The HSV, HSL, and OKLCH chips in the card header change what the area shows. With a thumb focused, arrow keys move one step, Shift + arrow ten, and Home, End, Page Up, and Page Down jump to the edges. Click Before to go back to the color you started from. Edit as gives number fields for RGB, HSL, HWB, OKLCH, or Lab plus alpha.
Formats
The active color as HEX, RGB, HSL, HWB, HSV, CMYK, Lab, LCH, OKLab, OKLCH, and its CSS name. Each row has a copy button; Copy all copies every row. The tiles below show black and white text on the color with their contrast.
Working palette
The palette you are building. Click a color to use it; hover or focus one to set it to the current color, rename it, or remove it. Drag to reorder, or press Alt + ← / → on a focused color; F2 renames and Delete removes. Sort (Undo brings your own order back), save to the library, or drop an image on the card (or use From image…) to pull its main colors.

Library

Palettes
Open a palette to edit it in the working palette; saving updates the palette it came from. Rename, duplicate, download, or delete from each card. Import… reads pasted text or a file (hex lists, CSS or SCSS variables, JSON, CSV, GIMP .gpl) and tells you how many values were not colors; you can also drop a palette file on this pane.
Saved Colors
Colors you starred. Click one to use it; double-click or F2 renames it and Delete removes it.
Recent
Colors you settled on, newest first. Turn recording off or change how many are kept in Settings.
Named Colors
The 148 CSS color names. Filter by name or hex, or turn on Nearest to current to see the closest names, and click one to use it.

Tools

Adjust
Lighten, darken, saturate, desaturate, rotate the hue, invert, grey out, complement, or make the active color opaque, in perceptual OKLCH steps. Turn on Apply to working palette to change every palette color at once.
Contrast
Checks text against a background with the WCAG 2 ratio and APCA, shows which levels pass, and can suggest the nearest passing text or background color. Palette grid compares every pair of working colors.
Harmonies
Complementary, analogous, triadic, split, tetradic, square, and monochromatic sets built from the active color, in OKLCH or HSL hues.
Shades & Tints
A 50–950 scale in the style of Tailwind, or tints and shades in even steps, each with its contrast against white and black. Copy the scale as CSS variables.
Mix
Blends the active color with a second color in a color space you choose and shows the steps between them.
Gradient
Linear, radial, or conic gradients with editable stops, ready to copy as CSS or download as SVG.
Color Vision
Shows how the working palette or the active color looks with protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, their milder forms, and achromatopsia, and flags colors that become hard to tell apart.
Export
Copies or downloads the working palette or your saved colors as CSS variables, SCSS, Less, Tailwind config, JSON, a hex list, CSV, GIMP .gpl, SVG, or PNG.

Most tools offer a way to send their results to the working palette or make one of them the active color.

Color formats you can type

Hex
#C9A84C, #c9a84c80, #CA5, with or without the #
RGB
rgb(201 168 76), rgb(201 168 76 / 50%), rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.5)
HSL / HWB
hsl(44 54% 54%), hsla(44, 54%, 54%, .5), hwb(44 30% 21%)
Lab / LCH
lab(70.53 4.79 50.96), lch(70.53 51.18 84.63)
OKLab / OKLCH
oklab(0.7432 0.001 0.117), oklch(74.3% 0.117 89.5deg)
color()
color(display-p3 0.77 0.66 0.35), also srgb and srgb-linear
CMYK
cmyk(0% 16% 62% 21%) or device-cmyk(…)
Names
Any of the 148 CSS names, such as goldenrod or rebeccapurple, plus transparent
Plain numbers
201, 168, 76 or 201 168 76 (0–255 red, green, blue)

Hue accepts deg, rad, grad, and turn. Colors outside what a screen can show (for example wide-gamut P3) are brought into range, and colorMan tells you when it did.

Keyboard shortcuts

Go to the color field
C

Undo the last color change

Esc

Single-letter shortcuts work whenever you are not typing in a field. While typing, Ctrl + Z undoes your typing instead of the color.

Your data

colorMan saves to this browser as you work; the dot in the toolbar shows when it has. Use Settings → Download backup to keep a copy or move it to another browser. Import & merge backup adds the backup's palettes and colors without replacing anything here, and asks before using its settings.
